# Plan

The design is in
[superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-design.md](superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-design.md).
The game ships in phases. Each phase is playable and live on Pages.

## Phase 1: one kart, live

One kart on the data-centre track. Gates, tickets, the pit lane and laps
(1, 3 or 10). Title, setup, countdown, HUD, pause and results screens. CI
and the Pages deploy. Detailed plan:
[superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-phase1.md](superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-phase1.md).

## Phase 1b: touch controls

On-screen controls for phones and tablets, after the desktop build is live.
Shipped in 0.2.0. Detailed plan:
[superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-touch.md](superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-touch.md).

## Phase 2: rivals, items, hazards

Seven bot rivals with their own tickets. Item crates and the five items.
The three hazards. Shipped in 0.3.0. Plan:
[superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-phase2.md](superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-phase2.md).

## Phase 3: sound and polish

Synthesized sound and an original tune. Art polish and a tuning pass.
Shipped in 0.4.0. Plan:
[superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-phase3.md](superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-oncall-kart-phase3.md).

## Shooter ("Zero Down")

A second game on the same site. The design is in
[superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-oncall-shooter-design.md](superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-oncall-shooter-design.md).

1. **Walk and fix:** the start screen that picks the game, the floor,
   movement, servers that break, fixing at the rack, patches, the 3-minute
   shift, the endings and the shift report. Shipped in 0.5.0. Plan:
   [superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-oncall-shooter-phase1.md](superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-oncall-shooter-phase1.md).
2. **Vendors and the money gun:** vendor roles, the slowdown, the gun,
   budget, lures, alarms, the toolkit. Shipped in 0.6.0. Plan:
   [superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-oncall-shooter-phase2.md](superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-oncall-shooter-phase2.md).
3. **Sound, touch and polish.**
